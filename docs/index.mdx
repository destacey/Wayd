# Welcome to Wayd

Wayd is an intelligent delivery management platform designed to give engineering leaders and teams end-to-end visibility into software delivery.

When delivery spans multiple teams, projects, and systems, visibility breaks down. Wayd brings it all together — tracking work items, aligning teams to planning intervals and products, and surfacing dependencies across the organization.

**Core Philosophy:** Wayd acts as a unified hub that synchronizes data from multiple business systems and combines it with capabilities those systems lack — connecting the dots so teams can see the full picture in one place.

## What Can You Do with Wayd?

Wayd provides a unified platform for organizations to:

- **Organize** — Structure teams into hierarchies of teams and teams of teams, track operating models and team memberships over time
- **Plan** — Define planning intervals and their iterations; manage objectives and risks across teams; build roadmaps, story maps, and run planning poker
- **Manage Work** — Track work items and sprints across workspaces with configurable work processes, workflows, and work types
- **Manage Portfolios** — Organize projects into portfolios and programs, track lifecycle status and strategic alignment
- **Align Strategy** — Define strategic themes that guide investment decisions and work prioritization
- **Manage Products** — Keep a catalog of what your organization owns, and track versions, releases, deployments, and delivery metrics
- **Integrate** — Synchronize work from Azure DevOps and people from Microsoft Entra ID and Workday, and connect Azure OpenAI for AI features
- **Connect AI assistants** — Let assistants such as Claude read and manage your delivery data through the [MCP server](./getting-started/mcp-server.mdx)
