# Home

The home page is a briefing on your work: what needs your attention today, what has changed on the records you follow or work on, and the projects, risks, planning intervals and team sprints you are part of.

It opens with a greeting and today's date, then a row of counts across the top, then the widgets in two columns: a wider main column and a narrower side column. On a tablet the side column's widgets sit two abreast below the main column; on a phone everything is one column, with the side column's widgets last.

The page appears straight away. Each widget loads on its own and shows a placeholder until its data arrives, so a slow widget never holds up the rest.

## What you see

You see only the widgets that apply to you. A widget needs some or all of:

- **A permission.** My projects needs permission to view projects, for example. Without it the widget is not shown.
- **A feature flag.** What's changed and Following need the **Following** feature flag, which an administrator turns on in **Settings → System → Feature Flags**.
- **An account linked to an employee.** Anything about "my" work — projects, risks, tasks, team sprints, notifications, the records you follow — comes from the employee record your account is linked to.

If your account is not linked to an employee, a banner at the top says so. Your projects, risks, tasks, team sprints, notifications and the records you follow appear once an administrator links your account in **Settings → Access → Users**.

Every widget is a card with its title at the top left and, where there is a full list behind it, **View all** at the top right. A widget that applies to you but has nothing to show says, in one line, how it fills — it does not disappear.

## Needs attention

The row of counts across the top shows up to four numbers that may need you today. Each one links to the list behind it, and its number turns orange or red only when it is above zero, so a row of plain numbers means nothing is waiting.

| Count | What it counts | Where it goes |
|-------|----------------|---------------|
| Unread notifications | Records with changes you have not read, the same count as the bell; shown as 99+ above 99 | Your [notifications](./notifications.mdx) |
| Overdue tasks (on your projects) | Open tasks on your approved and active projects whose planned end has passed | The [Projects Dashboard](./ppm/my-projects.mdx) |
| Due this week (on your projects) | Open tasks on those projects due by the end of this week (Saturday) | The Projects Dashboard |
| Risk follow-ups | Open risks assigned to you whose follow-up date is today or past | The My risks widget below |

A count shows only when its widget does: without the Following flag there is no unread count, and without permission to view risks there are no risk follow-ups. A count that fails to load shows a dash rather than a zero.

## Widgets

### Main column

- **What's changed** — your five latest [notifications](./notifications.mdx): what changed, on which record, and why you were told. Opening one goes to the record and marks it read. **View all** opens the full list.
- **My projects** — how many approved and active projects you hold a role on, broken down by role (Sponsor, Owner, PM, Member, Task Assignee). **View all** opens the Projects Dashboard.
- **My risks** — the open [risks](./planning/risks.mdx) assigned to you, with their exposure and follow-up date, soonest follow-up first. A follow-up that is today or past is highlighted.
### Side column

- **Planning intervals** — the [planning intervals](./planning/planning-intervals.mdx) running now and those starting in the next two weeks, with their dates, how many days are left (today included) or until they start, and links to Plan Review and the current iteration.
- **My team sprints** — the active sprint of each team you are a member of, one section per team.
- **Following** — the records you [follow](./following.mdx), most recently changed first. **View all** opens your Following list.
