# Notifications

The bell at the top right of every page tells you when something changes on a project, program or PI objective you [follow](./following.mdx) or work on. Notifications are behind the **Following** feature flag; an administrator turns it on in **Settings → Feature Flags**. They are in-app only. Wayd never sends them by email, Teams or push.

The bell needs your account to be linked to an employee. An account with no linked employee does not see it.

## The unread count

The number on the bell counts the records with something you have not read yet. Past 99 it shows **99+**. It refreshes about once a minute while Wayd's tab is in front, and each time you come back to it. Opening the bell refreshes it too.

## Read your notifications

Select the bell to see your newest notifications. On a phone or a narrow window they open full screen. **Unread** shows only what you have not read; **See all notifications** opens the full list, which you can also reach from your profile menu under **Notifications**. The full list loads more as you ask for it.

Each entry is one record, and it says:

- **What happened and who did it**, such as "Ada Lovelace changed the status of Apollo". When the record has changed more than once, the entry adds "and 2 other updates" rather than listing each one. When more than one of those changes is unread, the number beside the entry says how many.
- **Where**: the kind of record, its key, an objective's planning interval, how long ago, and the record's status now.
- **Why you were told**: "You follow this", "You work on this", or both.

| Change | Example |
|--------|---------|
| Created | Ada Lovelace created Apollo |
| Status changed | Ada Lovelace changed the status of Apollo |
| Lifecycle replaced | Ada Lovelace changed the lifecycle of Apollo |
| Health check added | Ada Lovelace reported health on Apollo |
| Dates changed | Ada Lovelace changed the dates of Apollo |
| Roles changed | Ada Lovelace changed the people on Apollo |
| Moved to another program or portfolio | Ada Lovelace moved Apollo |
| Scored | Ada Lovelace scored Apollo |

Entries use the record's name as it is now, so a renamed record never leaves an old name behind. A change made by someone whose account has no linked employee reads "Someone"; a change Wayd made with no person to name reads "Wayd".

## Mark notifications read

Opening the bell or the list marks nothing read. A notification is read when you:

- **Select an entry.** Every notification on that record is marked read, and the record opens.
- **Select the tick on an unread entry**, to mark its record read without opening it.
- **Select Mark all read**, in the bell or on the Notifications page.

You cannot delete a notification, only read it.

## Who is told

You are told about a change when you follow the record, and also when you work on it: you hold a role on a project or program, or you are on the team that owns a PI objective. If both are true you are told once, with both reasons. You are never told about your own changes, or about changes made by an import.

| Record | What tells you |
|--------|----------------|
| Project | Created, status changed, lifecycle replaced, health check added, dates changed, roles changed, moved, scored |
| Program | Created, status changed, dates changed, roles changed |
| PI objective | Created, status changed, health check added, dates changed |

Nobody can follow a record before it exists, so "Created" reaches only the people who work on it. A project's first status, the one it starts in, tells nobody.

You see a notification only while you can still see its record. If you lose access to a record, or it is deleted, its notifications leave your list and the unread count. Notifications are kept for 90 days.

## Mute notifications

Muting stops notifications without giving anything up. You can mute one record, or a whole reason.

Muting changes nothing else. You keep your access and your roles, and you still follow anything you followed. Only you can mute your own notifications, and you can mute only records you can see.

### Mute a record

Muting a record stops every notification about it, whatever the reason you would be told. You mute a record from a notification about it: select the muted-speaker button on the entry, in the bell or on the Notifications page. The confirmation stays until you select **Undo**, which unmutes it again, or **Dismiss**. If the record was already muted, nothing changes and there is nothing to undo.

To unmute a record, use the **Muted** tab (below).

Muting a record also marks its notifications read, so it leaves your unread count.

### Mute a reason

Muting a reason stops you being told for that reason on every record. Open **Notifications** from your profile menu, select the **Muted** tab, and switch a reason from **On** to **Muted**:

- **Records you follow**, to keep your Following list without being told about its changes.
- **Records you work on**, to stop hearing about records you hold a role on, or whose team you belong to.

You are still told when another reason applies. If you follow a project and also manage it, muting **Records you work on** still tells you about it, because you follow it.

### See everything you've muted

The **Muted** tab on the Notifications page shows each reason with whether it is muted and since when, and every record you have muted, newest first, with when you muted it and whether you still follow it. Select **Unmute** to hear about a record again. A record you can no longer see is not listed.

On the [Following](./following.mdx) page, a muted record shows a muted-speaker icon, and **Muted** in its detail.

### Unmuting

Unmuting tells you about changes from then on. It does not bring back anything that happened while the record or reason was muted; the record's **Activity** still shows every change.

An assistant connected to Wayd's [hosted MCP endpoint](../getting-started/mcp-server.mdx) can also list your notifications, mark them read, and mute or unmute records and reasons for you.
