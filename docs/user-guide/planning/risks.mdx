# Risks

A **Risk** is the potential that a circumstance could arise that alters the outcome of a project, objective, or work item. Risks are tracked at the [team](../organizations/index.mdx#teams) level and within [Planning Intervals](./planning-intervals.mdx).

Every risk has:
- **Summary** and optional **Description**
- **Team** — The [team](../organizations/index.mdx#teams) the risk belongs to, chosen when the risk is created; it cannot be changed afterwards
- **Reported By** — The employee who identified the risk: you, when you create it
- **Reported On** — When the risk was identified: the moment it was created
- **Status** — Open or Closed. A new risk is Open; change it with **Edit**
- **Category** (ROAM) — How the risk is being handled:
  - **Resolved** — The risk has been eliminated; no further action needed
  - **Owned** — Someone is actively managing the risk
  - **Accepted** — The risk is acknowledged and accepted as-is
  - **Mitigated** — Actions have been taken to reduce impact or likelihood
- **Impact** — Severity if the risk materializes (Low, Medium, High)
- **Likelihood** — Probability the risk will occur (Low, Medium, High)
- **Exposure** — Automatically calculated from Impact + Likelihood:

| | Low Impact | Medium Impact | High Impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Low Likelihood** | Low | Low | Medium |
| **Medium Likelihood** | Low | Medium | High |
| **High Likelihood** | Medium | High | High |

- **Assignee** — Person responsible for managing the risk
- **Follow-Up Date** — When to review the risk next
- **Response** — Planned mitigation actions
- **Closed Date** — Automatically set when status changes to Closed, and cleared when the risk is reopened

## Where Risks Appear

Risks are visible in multiple places:

- **[PI Risks section](./planning-intervals.mdx#pi-detail-page)** — The risks of the PI's teams that were reported before the PI ended and not closed before it started. Open risks only, unless **Include Closed** is switched on. Grid columns: Key, Summary, Team, Category, Exposure, Follow Up Date, Assignee, Reported On, and Status while closed risks are included. **Hide Team** removes the Team column.
- **[Team Risks section](../organizations/index.mdx#risks)** — Risks for a specific team, with the same toggle and create capability.
- **[Plan Review](./planning-intervals.mdx#plan-review)** — Within each team's view during PI planning ceremonies.
- **Home** — **My Assigned Risks** lists the open risks assigned to you, with their follow-up dates. It shows only when you have some.

## Risk Detail Page

Each risk has its own page, with two sections:

- **Risk** — The ROAM category, highlighted among the four; the **Description** and **Response**; and an **Evaluation** matrix placing the risk by impact and likelihood
- **Activity** — The risk's recorded history

The risk's exposure shows as a tag beside its name. The details panel shows the **Assignee**, the **Follow-Up Date** — with how many days remain, or how many days it is overdue, while the risk is open — **Reported By**, **Reported On**, **Closed**, and the team. **Edit** changes everything but the team.

## Common Tasks

### Creating a Risk

1. Navigate to a PI's Risks section, or a team's Risks section
2. Click **Create Risk**
3. Choose the **Team** and enter **Summary**, **Category**, **Impact**, and **Likelihood**
4. Optionally add a **Description**, an **Assignee**, a **Follow Up** date, and a **Response**

Creating a risk needs the **Create Risks** permission and your account to be linked to an employee, since the risk records who reported it. Changing one needs **Update Risks**.

### Managing Risks

- Update **Category** as risk handling evolves (e.g., from Owned to Mitigated)
- Update **Status** to Closed when the risk is resolved — the Closed Date is set automatically
- Use the **Follow-Up Date** to schedule periodic reviews
- Use the **Include Closed** toggle to review historical risks
- Open a risk's **Activity** section to see how it was handled over time: edits, ROAM category changes, reassessments, reassignments, follow-up dates, and when it was closed or reopened. See [Activity Log](../activity-log.mdx).
