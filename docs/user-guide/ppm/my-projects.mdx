# Projects Dashboard

The **Projects Dashboard** (**PPM > Projects Dashboard**) shows the [projects](./projects.mdx) a person is involved in and leads with what needs attention. It replaces the earlier My Projects dashboard; the old address forwards here.

## Scope

The scope decides whose projects the page shows:

- **Me** — projects where your linked employee holds a role. This is the default, and it is missing when your account has no employee record.
- **Person** — projects for any employee, chosen from the picker. Use it to see what someone you lead is carrying. The chosen person is kept in the address bar, so the view can be shared as a link.

Within the scope, the **Role** chips narrow the list to the roles the person holds (Sponsor, Owner, PM, Member, Task Assignee; all by default) and the **Status** chips narrow by project status (Approved and Active by default). **Reset** restores the defaults and **Refresh** reloads.

## Attention tiles

The row of tiles under the scope bar is a triage summary of the projects in scope. Click a tile to keep only those projects in the list below; click it again to clear.

| Tile | Counts |
|------|--------|
| In scope | Every project matching the scope and filters |
| Unhealthy | Projects whose current [health check](./projects.mdx#project-health-checks) is Unhealthy |
| At risk | Projects whose current health check is At Risk |
| Overdue tasks | Open [tasks](./projects.mdx#project-tasks) past their planned end, with how many projects they sit on |
| No health check | Open projects with no current health check, whether never reported or expired |
| Ending in 30 days | Open projects whose planned end falls within the next 30 days |

Task counts follow the same visibility rule as the project plan: on a project where the person holds a leadership role (Sponsor, Owner or PM) every task counts, otherwise only the tasks assigned to them.

## The list

Projects are grouped by **Portfolio**, **Program**, **Health** or **Status**. Each group header shows its count and a one-line summary (unhealthy, at risk, overdue tasks), and collapses on click. Within a group the sort is **Health, then overdue** by default, or Name, End date or Score. The search box matches the project name or key.

Each row shows:

- Current health, with the health check details on hover
- Key, name, and the portfolio and program underneath
- Status
- **Stages** — every [lifecycle stage](./projects.mdx#project-stages) as a step: completed, in progress, or not started. Several stages can be in progress at once; hover a step for its status and progress.
- Open task counts: overdue and due this week, or the upcoming count when nothing is more urgent
- Sponsors, Owners and PMs as avatars
- Planned end date, highlighted when it falls within the next 30 days
- Current [score](../settings/scoring-models.mdx), when the project has been scored
- The scoped person's roles on the project

Click a row to open the project's details in a drawer, with links to the full project page and its plan.
