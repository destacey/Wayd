# Sprints & Metrics

## Sprints

A **Sprint** (also called an Iteration in some contexts) is a short time-boxed period — typically 2 weeks — during which a team implements and delivers [work items](./work-items.mdx#work-items).

Every sprint has:
- **Name** — Sprint identifier
- **Type** — Sprint or Iteration
- **State** — Future, Active, or Completed
- **Date Range** — The first and last planned days, as calendar dates. The sprint includes the whole of its last day
- **Team** — The owning [team](../organizations/index.mdx#teams) (optional)
- **Ownership** — Owned (by Wayd) or Managed (from external system like Azure DevOps)

Managed sprints may have **External Metadata** such as the Azure DevOps iteration path.

A sprint's state follows its dates, and Wayd works it out whenever the sprint is shown, so it is never out of date between syncs:
- **Future** — before the sprint starts.
- **Active** — from the start until the end. A sprint starts when the team [starts it](#starting-completing-and-reopening-a-sprint), or else at the beginning of its first planned day. It ends when the team completes it, or else at the end of its last planned day, so a sprint is Active for the whole of its last day unless it was completed earlier.
- **Completed** — after the end.

Days are counted in the team's time zone, from its [operating model](../organizations/index.mdx#operating-model). A team has one Active sprint at a time: a sprint becomes Active only once the previous one has ended, and starting the next sprint ends the previous one. A sprint whose team is not mapped to a Wayd team follows its planned days in the system default time zone.

For example, take a sprint planned to start on Monday that the team starts on Tuesday morning. Once the start is recorded, the sprint is Future on Monday and Active from Tuesday morning, which is what its timeline, history and metrics use. Until someone presses Start, Wayd has only the planned dates to go on, so on the Monday itself the sprint shows as Active.

**Timelines, countdowns and health follow the days a sprint is Active**, not its planned dates. On the sprint page, the **My Team Sprints** cards on Home and the sprint cards on a PI iteration, the timeline bar, **Days Remaining** and the health tag run from the day the sprint became Active to its last Active day: day 1 is the day the team started it, or else its first planned day, and the last day is the day it was completed, or else its last planned day. The date range shown beside a sprint stays the planned one. A sprint whose team is not mapped to a Wayd team uses its planned dates.

Sprints are [mapped to PI iterations](../planning/planning-intervals.mdx#sprint-mapping) to connect team delivery cadence to the broader planning schedule.

### Starting, Completing and Reopening a Sprint

A sprint's synced dates are what was **planned**. Teams often start or finish on a different day — the planned Monday is a holiday, so they plan on the Friday before — so a team records when it **actually** started and completed the sprint in Wayd. Nothing is written back to Azure DevOps, and syncing never changes these dates.

| Action | What it records | When it is allowed |
|---|---|---|
| **Start** | The moment you choose — now by default, or earlier — as the sprint's actual start | After the team's previous sprint started (and not before it was completed, if it was), up to the end of the sprint's last planned day, and before the next sprint's start. The team's first sprint can be started up to 3 days before its planned start. |
| **Complete** | The moment you choose — now by default, or earlier — as the sprint's actual end | After the sprint started — pressing Start is not required, its commitment point having passed is enough — before a later sprint has started, and once any earlier sprint the team left open has been completed |
| **Reopen** | Clears the actual end | While the team has not started a later sprint |

The dialog offers only the moments the rules allow, so a recorded start or end never overlaps the team's other sprints. A moment can't be in the future. Activity records both the moment you chose and when you recorded it.

**A team has one open sprint at a time.** If the previous sprint is still open when you start the next one, Wayd asks you to confirm completing it. On confirm, the previous sprint is completed at the same moment the new one starts.

**When the team doesn't record a date.** If the team doesn't start or complete the sprint in Wayd, the sprint follows its planned dates: it starts at the beginning of its first planned day and ends at the end of its last, counted in the team's time zone from the [operating model](../organizations/index.mdx#operating-model) in effect on the sprint's planned start. The only exception is when it meets a neighbouring sprint: a sprint doesn't start until the previous one has ended, and where the planned dates overlap it ends when the next one starts (see below).

The sprint page shows the **Planned start** and **Planned end** beside the **Actual start** and **Actual end** the team recorded, which read **—** until they are recorded. Recorded times are shown in your time zone. **Length** counts the days the sprint actually runs: its planned days, moved by any recorded start or end.

**Commitment point.** Metrics take the team's commitment when the team starts the sprint, or, if it doesn't, at the end of the team's [commitment grace period](../organizations/index.mdx#operating-model) after the planned start — with the default of 1 day, the end of the first planned day. That leaves the team its first day to plan: work added on that day counts as committed, not as added scope.

**Overlapping sprints in Azure DevOps.** Azure DevOps lets one team's iterations overlap, and Wayd keeps the source dates as they are. Where they overlap, a sprint the team hasn't completed ends when the next sprint starts rather than on its planned end, so the team's sprints never overlap. The sprint page and the team's Sprints section show a warning for overlapping sprints so they can be corrected at the source.

**Who can do it.** Members of the sprint's team, or of the team of teams it belongs to, who hold the `Permissions.Iterations.Update` permission. Holders of `Permissions.Iterations.Administer` can act on any team's sprints. Sprints whose Azure DevOps team is not mapped to a Wayd team cannot be started or completed. See [Permissions](../settings/permissions.mdx#sprint-lifecycle-team-membership).

**Moving a sprint to another team.** If Azure DevOps moves a sprint to a different team — for example after an area or iteration path changes — the sprint keeps the actual dates its team recorded. If it was open and the team it moved to already has an open sprint, the moved sprint is completed at the time of the sync, so the team still has one open sprint.

Every start, completion and reopening is recorded in the sprint's [Activity](../activity-log.mdx).

### Correcting Actual Dates

Past sprints — including every sprint from before Wayd recorded actual dates — have no actual start or end, and a team that forgot to press Start or Complete needs to fix the record afterwards. **Correct Actual Dates** in the sprint's **Actions** menu sets the actual start and end to the moments you choose, on any sprint, past or current. Clear a value to have the sprint [follow its planned date](#starting-completing-and-reopening-a-sprint) again.

The same rules as Start and Complete keep the team's sprints in order:
- A start falls after the previous sprint's start and before the end of the sprint's last planned day. The team's first sprint can start up to 3 days before its planned start.
- An end falls after the sprint's start, recorded or planned.
- A sprint's end can't pass the next sprint's start, recorded or planned, so the team's sprints never overlap.
- A sprint left open (started but not completed) can't be followed by a sprint with actual dates.
- Neither date can be in the future.

**Correcting neighbouring sprints together.** The dialog also shows the previous and next sprints. To move this sprint's end past the next sprint's start — say the team finished a day late — correct the next sprint's start in the same dialog. Both are checked and saved together, and only the sprints you changed are saved.

A correction moves the sprint's commitment point, which changes its metrics, so each one is recorded in the sprint's [Activity](../activity-log.mdx) with the dates before and after. Who can correct is the same as who can start and complete.

### Sprint Detail Page

The sprint detail page shows:
- **Actions** — Start, Complete and Reopen, when you may take them (see [above](#starting-completing-and-reopening-a-sprint)), and [Correct Actual Dates](#correcting-actual-dates)
- **Planned and actual dates** — the planned start and end, the actual start and end the team recorded (**—** until recorded), and the sprint's length in days
- **Team** link and timeline progress bar
- **Sprint Metrics** (for Active/Completed sprints) — see [Sprint Metrics](#sprint-metrics) below
- **Backlog grid** — [Work items](./work-items.mdx#work-items) in the sprint with Rank, Key, Type, Title, the team's estimate ([Story Points, Effort or Size](./work-items.mdx#estimate-columns)), Status, Status Category, and Team
- **Activity** — the sprint's recorded history: what changed, who changed it, and when. See [Activity Log](../activity-log.mdx)
- **Links** section

A **Switch Sprint** dropdown lets you quickly navigate between other sprints on the same team.

### Sprints List Page

The sprints list (**Work Management > Sprints**) shows all sprints across all [teams](../organizations/index.mdx#teams) in a grid with Key, Name, Team, State, Start Date, End Date, Started and Completed, sorted by most recent start date. Started and Completed show the actual moments the team recorded, and are empty until the team records them.

Teams also see their sprints in the [Sprints section](../organizations/index.mdx#sprints) of their detail page.

### Active Team Sprint Card

If you are a [team member](../organizations/index.mdx#team-members) of one or more teams, the **Home** page shows a **My Team Sprints** panel in the right column with an active sprint card for each of your teams (Teams only — Teams of Teams do not have sprints). The cards are sorted by team code.

Each card shows:
- **Team code** (links to the team page) and **sprint name** (links to the sprint page)
- **Sprint health indicator**
- **Timeline progress** — Days elapsed and percentage complete
- **Completion Rate**, **Velocity**, **In Progress**, and **Avg Cycle Time** metrics
- **Predictability** — If the sprint is mapped to a Planning Interval and the team has objectives, a predictability card appears per PI (links to the team's plan review page)

The panel is only visible if you have at least one active team sprint.

## Sprint Metrics

Sprint metrics provide quantitative insight into a sprint's progress and delivery. Metrics are available for both individual sprints and [PI iterations](../planning/planning-intervals.mdx#pi-iteration-detail-page) (which aggregate across all mapped sprints).

**Metrics are measured in the team's sizing method.** A sprint uses the [sizing method](../organizations/index.mdx#operating-model) of the operating model in effect on its planned start — Story Points, Effort, Size or Count — so a team that changes how it sizes keeps its earlier sprints in the old unit. A sprint whose team is not mapped to a Wayd team counts work items. A selector switches the metrics between the team's sizing method and counting work items; for a team that sizes by count the two are the same, so the selector is disabled.

### Metric Definitions

| Metric | Formula | Description |
|--------|---------|-------------|
| **Total** | Sum of every work item's estimate in the team's sizing method (or the number of work items) | Everything committed to the sprint |
| **Completed** | Items where status category is **Done** or **Removed** | Work that is finished or removed from scope |
| **In Progress** | Items where status category is **Active** | Work currently being performed |
| **Not Started** | Items where status category is **Proposed** | Work that hasn't begun yet |
| **Remaining** | Total − Completed | Work still to be done |
| **Completion Rate** | `(Completed / Total) × 100` | Percentage of sprint work completed. Target: 80% |
| **Velocity** | Completed estimate (or the number of completed work items) | How much work the team delivered. Percentage of total shown alongside. |
| **Avg Cycle Time** | `Sum(Done − Activated) / Count` for Done items with both timestamps | Average days from start to completion. Only includes items that moved through Active to Done. |
| **Unestimated** | Count of items with no value in the team's sizing method | Indicates estimation gaps. Highlighted red if any exist. An estimate of 0 is an estimate, and another estimate never stands in: a Story Points team's item with only an Effort is unestimated. Not shown when counting work items. |

### How "Completed" Is Defined

**Completed** includes both **Done** and **Removed** [status categories](./work-configuration.mdx#work-status-categories). Removed items are counted as completed because they represent work that has been resolved (even if by removal from scope) and is no longer in progress.

This means:
- `Remaining = Total − (Done + Removed)`
- `Completion Rate = (Done + Removed) / Total × 100`

### Cycle Time in Sprint Context

**Average Cycle Time** is only calculated from work items that:
1. Have a **Done** status category (not Removed)
2. Have both an **Activated Timestamp** and a **Done Timestamp**
3. The Done timestamp is after the Activated timestamp

```
Per item: Cycle Time = Done Timestamp − Activated Timestamp (in days)
Sprint average: Sum(all item cycle times) / Count(eligible Done items)
```

Items that were moved directly to Done without being Activated, or items that were Removed, are excluded from the cycle time calculation.

For a deeper analysis of cycle time patterns over longer periods, use the [Cycle Time Report](./cycle-time-report.mdx) on individual team or employee pages.

### PI Iteration Metric Aggregation

When viewing a [PI iteration](../planning/planning-intervals.mdx#pi-iteration-detail-page), metrics are aggregated from all mapped team sprints:
- **Counts** are summed across all sprints (Total, Completed, In Progress, Not Started, Unestimated)
- **Estimates** are summed only when every sprint is measured in the same sizing method. When teams size in different units, estimates are never added together: the iteration's totals are counts, and each team's sprint card shows its own unit
- **Average Cycle Time** is the average of each sprint's average cycle time (simple average, not weighted)
- **Team Count** and **Sprint Count** show how many teams and sprints are included
